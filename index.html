          Where Backstory's information comes from
          
  
          
    
          Wikipedia
          The short background summaries, quoted with a link to the full article. Text is shared under CC BY-SA 4.0.
          
    
          Open Library
          Books, from the Internet Archive's open catalog. Some can be borrowed free with an Open Library account.
          
    
          OpenAlex
          Journal articles, book chapters, and scholarly books, with citation counts and links to free copies. If OpenAlex is busy, Backstory uses Crossref, the DOI registry.
          
    
          DOAJ
          The Directory of Open Access Journals: articles anyone can read free.
          
    
          Jev (TypeSafe AI)
          When "Rank with Jev" is on, Jev reads each catalog entry and returns numbers: how related it is, its angle, its reading level, and whether it argues one side. Rules in this page turn those numbers into the ranking and the sentences you see. Jev doesn't write any text.
          
    
          Gemini
          Only if you choose to paste a packet into it. Backstory sends nothing to Gemini.
          
  
          
  
          What leaves your browser: your search words go to the catalogs above, and short catalog entries plus the first part of what you brought in go to Jev through a proxy that stores nothing. Your folders, library, and notes stay in this browser.
